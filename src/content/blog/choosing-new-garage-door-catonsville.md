Replacing a garage door is both a functional and curb-appeal decision. For Catonsville and Arbutus homes, insulation and durability matter.

## Key factors

- **Insulation (R-value)**: Higher R-value helps with energy efficiency if the garage is attached or finished.
- **Material**: Insulated steel is popular for strength and low maintenance. Carriage-house and wood-look styles add character.
- **Wind and weather**: Quality weather seals and proper installation reduce drafts and water entry.
- **Opener compatibility**: Newer doors often pair best with modern belt-drive openers for quiet operation.
