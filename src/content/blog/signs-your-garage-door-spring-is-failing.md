Garage door springs do the heavy lifting. When they wear out, the door becomes hard to open, unbalanced, or can fail suddenly with a loud bang.

## Common warning signs

1. **Door feels heavier than usual** — You have to help the opener or lift more by hand.
2. **Loud bang or snap** — Often a torsion spring breaking.
3. **Door opens unevenly or on an angle** — Can indicate spring or cable issues.
4. **Visible gaps or rust on the spring** — Visual wear.
5. **Opener strains or reverses** — Motor working against a failing spring.
