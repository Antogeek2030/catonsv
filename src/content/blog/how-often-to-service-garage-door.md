A well-maintained garage door lasts longer and operates more safely. Maryland weather — humidity, freeze-thaw cycles, and temperature swings — affects springs, weather seals, and lubricants.

## Recommended schedule

- **Every 6–12 months**: Visual inspection of springs, cables, rollers, and tracks. Lubricate hinges, rollers, and springs with a garage-door-safe lubricant.
- **Annually**: Test balance (disconnect opener and lift door by hand — it should stay roughly halfway). Check weather seals and safety reverse.
- **As needed**: After storms, impacts, or if you notice noise, sticking, or slow operation.

Tracks and rollers, spring condition and balance, cable integrity, opener force and sensors, remote/keypad function, and weather sealing.
